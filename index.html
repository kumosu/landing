Your app needs a server? Don't ask users to wrestle with Docker. Using the SDK, your app deploys itself into the user's cloud. We share revenue from managed instances to support developers of sustainable apps.

Your data is never a hostage — migrate from managed to self-hosted anytime. We're building an open standard and a free BYOC cloud implementation to bring this freedom to everyone.
